- question: Clamp or grommet mount — which should I choose?
  answer: A clamp mount works on most desks with a rear edge you can grip and is easier to remove or reposition. A grommet mount needs a drilled hole but sits slightly more securely and suits desks without a usable rear edge, like some standing desks with a modesty panel.
- question: How do I know if a monitor arm will support my monitor's weight?
  answer: Check the arm's weight range against your monitor's actual weight (found in its spec sheet, not guessed), and check the VESA mount pattern (commonly 75x75mm or 100x100mm) matches what your monitor supports before buying.
- question: Is gas-spring adjustment actually worth it over a fixed arm?
  answer: Yes if you adjust height or angle regularly through the day — gas-spring arms hold position smoothly with one hand. A fixed-position arm is fine if you'll set it once and rarely touch it again.

An arm that slowly droops under a monitor's weight defeats the entire point. Here's what to check before buying, and which ones actually hold position.

## What actually matters

- **A genuinely secure clamp or grommet mount** — check reviews specifically for desk-edge compatibility with your desk's thickness and material.
- **Weight range that comfortably covers your monitor** — check the actual spec, don't estimate; buying with margin avoids sagging over time.
- **VESA mount compatibility** — confirm your monitor's VESA pattern matches before ordering, since this isn't universally standard.
- **Smooth gas-spring adjustment if you'll reposition often** — a fixed arm is fine for a set-and-forget setup.
