A standing desk that wobbles at full height or takes 30 seconds to adjust just doesn't get used. Here's what to check, and which ones hold up under daily use.

## What actually matters

- **Genuine stability at full standing height** — check reviews specifically for wobble complaints, not just weight capacity numbers.
- **Electric adjustment with memory presets** if you'll actually switch positions during the day — a slow manual crank often means it never gets used.
- **Desktop size matched to your actual setup** — measure your monitor and keyboard footprint before assuming a size will work.
- **Cable management** — a genuinely annoying afterthought on cheaper desks; worth checking if grommets or a cable tray are included.
