- question: Cable tray or cable sleeve — which do I actually need?
  answer: A cable tray mounted under the desk is better for gathering multiple cables and a power strip out of sight, while a cable sleeve is better for bundling a visible run of cables (like a monitor-to-PC run) into one neat tube. Most setups benefit from using both together.

A desk setup with cables draped everywhere looks messy and makes cleaning genuinely harder. Here's what actually keeps things tidy, and what holds up.

## What actually matters

- **A tray that actually fits your desk's underside clearance** — measure before buying rather than assuming.
- **Sleeve diameter matched to your actual cable bundle** — too tight and it's a hassle to route cables through; too loose and it looks bulky.
- **Reliable adhesive on clips**, if using them — check recent reviews specifically for adhesive failing over time.
- **Room for a power strip**, if that's part of what you're hiding — not every tray is sized for one.
