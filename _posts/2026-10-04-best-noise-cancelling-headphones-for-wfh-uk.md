faq:
- question: Do I need active noise cancelling, or is a decent seal enough?
  answer: For most WFH noise — a dishwasher, traffic, someone else's call in the next room — active noise cancelling (ANC) makes a bigger difference than passive isolation alone, since it electronically cancels low, constant frequencies that a seal around the ear can't block as effectively. A simple closed-back pair without ANC still helps, but it won't touch the hum of a washing machine or a busy street the way ANC does.
- question: Will the microphone on ANC headphones be good enough for work calls?
  answer: It depends on the model. Headphones built around music listening, where ANC is the headline feature, often have a workable but unremarkable microphone, fine for internal calls but not ideal for client-facing ones. If call quality matters more than audio quality, check for models with multiple mics and dedicated voice-pickup processing rather than a single mic designed mainly for picking up your own ANC algorithm's feedback.
- question: Over-ear or on-ear — does it matter for working at a desk all day?
  answer: Over-ear headphones (where the cushion surrounds the ear) are almost always more comfortable for multi-hour desk use and seal out more noise, since the cup doesn't press directly on the ear itself. On-ear designs are lighter and more packable but tend to clamp harder over a full day, which shows up as discomfort well before most work-from-home sessions end.

Open-plan living means someone else's call, the dishwasher, or traffic noise is never far from your desk. A good pair of noise-cancelling headphones deals with all three at once, and the gap between a budget pair and a premium one is smaller than the price difference suggests for anyone who isn't an audiophile.

## What actually matters

- **ANC strength on low, constant noise** — active noise cancelling is most effective against steady, low-frequency sound like traffic, fans, and appliances; it's far less effective against sudden sharp noises like a door slamming or someone talking right next to you.
- **Microphone quality for calls** — a headphone's ANC and its microphone are separate engineering problems; check reviews for call quality specifically rather than assuming great music ANC means a great work mic.
- **All-day comfort over a full shift** — clamping force and ear cup padding matter more after four hours than they do in a five-minute shop test; over-ear designs with memory foam tend to hold up better for back-to-back meetings.
- **Battery life and charging** — most ANC headphones now last 20-40+ hours per charge, but a USB-C quick-charge option is worth having so a forgotten overnight charge doesn't derail a morning of calls.
- **Multipoint Bluetooth** — the ability to stay paired to both a laptop and a phone at once avoids fumbling with Bluetooth settings between a work call and a personal one.
