- question: Do I actually need a dedicated desk lamp if my room already has overhead lighting?
  answer: Usually yes. Overhead lighting creates a flat, even wash across the whole room but rarely gives enough direct brightness on your desk surface for reading or detailed work, and it can throw glare or reflections onto a screen. A desk lamp lets you add focused, adjustable light exactly where you need it without over-lighting the rest of the room.
- question: What colour temperature should I look for in a desk lamp?
  answer: Most good desk lamps let you adjust between warm (around 2700-3000K) and cool white (5000-6500K) light, or settle on a neutral daylight tone in between. Cooler light tends to suit focused screen work and video calls, while warmer light is easier on the eyes later in the day. Adjustable colour temperature matters more than picking one fixed tone.
- question: Base lamp or clamp lamp — which is better for a home office?
  answer: A base lamp sits freely on the desk and is easier to reposition, but takes up desk space. A clamp lamp attaches to the edge of your desk or shelf and frees up the surface entirely, which matters more on a small or shared desk. Neither is more "proper" than the other; it comes down to how much desk space you actually have.

Overhead lighting rarely does your desk any favours — it's either too dim for close work or glary enough to show up on a video call. A proper desk lamp fixes both, and the right one depends more on your desk space and habits than on price.

## What actually matters

- **Adjustable brightness and colour temperature** — the ability to shift between warm and cool white (and dim it down) matters more than raw maximum brightness.
- **Base vs clamp mounting** — a freestanding base is easy to reposition but takes up desk space; a clamp frees the surface entirely but is fixed once attached.
- **Glare and flicker control** — diffused, flicker-free LEDs reduce eye strain over a full working day far more than a high lumen count alone.
- **Extra ports and features** — a built-in USB charging port is genuinely useful for topping up a phone or earbuds; app or voice control is a nice-to-have, not essential for a basic task light.
